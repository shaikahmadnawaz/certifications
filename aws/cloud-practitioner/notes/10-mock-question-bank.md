# AWS Cloud Practitioner Mock Question Bank

## What is the Mock Question Bank? & Why It Matters

This question bank is for scenario recognition, elimination practice, and weak-area discovery. The goal is not just to know the answer, but to know why the other options are worse.

Use it in sets of 20, then review misses by domain using `07-official-objective-mapping.md`.

---
